Asset Location Optimizer: The right asset in the right account.

The wrong location can erase a third of the compounding curve through tax drag alone. Each asset class has a natural home in the architecture based on its yield, turnover, and expected return.

Asset Location Map

The rule of thumb. High-yield assets that produce ordinary-income taxable distributions (bonds, Real Estate Investment Trusts) belong in tax-deferred accounts where the income isn't taxed annually. High-growth assets with the longest expected runway (international equities, small-cap growth) belong in Roth accounts where the future tax-free withdrawal matters most. Tax-efficient assets (broad domestic index funds with low turnover, qualified dividends) belong in taxable brokerage where they create the least annual drag.

Roth Conversion Planner: Convert traditional balances at the right tax rate.

The Roth Conversion ladder is the highest-leverage tax move in the post-walk-away window. In the years your taxable income drops (the gap between W-2 ending and Social Security/RMDs starting), convert traditional balances to Roth at lower bracket rates. The conversion lifetime is one of the biggest swings in the entire architecture.

When the conversion window opens. The cleanest window is the gap between your W-2 ending and your Social Security or Required Minimum Distributions starting.
